ON YOUR HOME SCREEN

Take My Journey with you.

Your daily workouts, muscle guides and checklist, in an app you can open from your Home Screen. No App Store needed.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). You may find it in Safari’s page menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Can’t see Add to Home Screen? Choose Edit Actions in the Share menu to add it.

The workout plan and muscle guides are saved for offline use after the app loads online. Videos need internet. Your checklist stays on this device; it does not sync between Safari and the installed app. Journal entries are the latest published snapshot, not a live chat connection.

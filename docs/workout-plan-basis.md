# Gym starter plan — reviewed October 12, 2026

The user reports gym access without movement limitations, walking/running and a short lifting session. Consistent training history, working weights, exercise tolerance and gym machine models are unknown. The prescription therefore begins with one working set and offers a deliberate move to two sets. Selecting a volume is a preference, not evidence of completing training.

## Evidence and programming decisions

- [ACSM 2026 position stand summary](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/): train all major muscle groups at least twice weekly. Failure training, equipment type and complex periodization are not consistently essential for general outcomes. Two repeated full-body days are an implementation choice for learning and adherence; this is not an optimized hypertrophy program or an ACSM-endorsed personal prescription.
- [CDC adult activity guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html): work toward 150 moderate minutes weekly and at least two strength days. The program’s moderate totals exclude warm-ups, cool-downs and optional easy walking. Start/Build/Goal prescribe 65/105/150 moderate minutes; stages are selected by tolerance, never advanced automatically with the calendar.
- [Mayo Clinic technique guidance](https://sportsmedicine.mayoclinic.org/news/weight-training-dos-and-donts-of-proper-technique/): controlled form, manageable resistance, warm-up and days away from lifting the same muscles. Starting volume and avoiding maximal loads reflect the limited reported training history.
- [NASM exercise library](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library): leg press, machine chest press, close-grip seated row, seated curl and plank mechanics/muscle lists. [ACE seated overhead press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/45/seated-overhead-press/) informs shoulder-press technique. Source URLs and original video creators are retained per exercise in `site/workout-data.js`.

The six strength exercises cover lower-body extension, horizontal push/pull, knee flexion, overhead push and trunk stability. Arm work is included through presses and rows. The seated row replaces unilateral dumbbell rows to simplify the routine and avoid doubling sets by side. A guided machine is chosen for setup simplicity, not a claim of superior gains or universal safety. Ask gym staff to match the demonstrated setup to the local machine.

## One-hour budget

At two sets: strength blocks = 6 + 6 + 6 + 5 + 5 + 4 = 32 minutes. These include repetitions, prescribed rests and light rehearsal sets. Add 5 warm-up + 10 or 15 cardio + 3 cool-down + 5 changing stations = 55 or 60 minutes. With one set, blocks are reduced to 17 minutes, making sessions 40 or 45 minutes. Cardio days = 5 warm-up + 15/25/40 moderate walking + 3 cool-down + 2 setup = 25/35/50 minutes. Saturday is optional 20-minute easy walking; Sunday has no required workout.

Minute allocations are estimates. Queues, unfamiliar equipment, longer rests and video study can extend a session. Shorten the final cardio block to respect the hour; do not rush technique or remove needed rest. No recommendation to fill recovery days with an hour of activity. No exercise calorie burn is inferred or added to the food budget.

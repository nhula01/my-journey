Main plan: three compound sets and two assistance/core sets, three nonconsecutive lifting days. A two-set option supports a lighter week. ## Evidence and programming decisions

- [ACSM 2026 position stand summary](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/): train all major muscle groups at least twice weekly. Failure training, equipment type and complex periodization are not consistently essential for general outcomes. Three repeated full-body days are a practical prescription for the reported experience. Larger movements receive nine working sets/week; hamstrings, shoulders and core receive six direct sets. Assisting muscle work is not counted as an equal direct set. ACSM’s roughly ten sets/week hypertrophy guide is not a universal minimum or guarantee; volume may be reviewed after actual performance and recovery.
- [CDC adult activity guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html): work toward 150 moderate minutes weekly and at least two strength days. The program’s moderate totals exclude warm-ups, cool-downs and optional easy walking. Start/Build/Goal prescribe 60/90/150 moderate minutes; stages are selected by tolerance, never advanced automatically with the calendar.
- [Mayo Clinic technique guidance](https://sportsmedicine.mayoclinic.org/news/weight-training-dos-and-donts-of-proper-technique/): controlled form, manageable resistance, warm-up and days away from lifting the same muscles. Select challenging loads with approximately 1–3 clean repetitions left; avoid guessing a 1RM from the reported experience.
- [NASM exercise library](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library): leg press, machine chest press, close-grip seated row, seated curl and plank mechanics/muscle lists. [ACE seated overhead press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/45/seated-overhead-press/) informs shoulder-press technique. Source URLs and original video creators are retained per exercise in `site/workout-data.js`.

The six strength exercises cover lower-body extension, horizontal push/pull, knee flexion, overhead push and trunk stability. Arm work is included through presses and rows. The seated row replaces unilateral dumbbell rows to simplify the routine and avoid doubling sets by side. A guided machine is chosen for setup simplicity, not a claim of superior gains or universal safety. Ask gym staff to match the demonstrated setup to the local machine.

## One-hour budget

Main plan: strength blocks = 9 + 9 + 9 + 5 + 5 + 4 = 41 minutes, including rests and light rehearsal sets. Add 5 warm-up + 5 moderate cardio + 3 cool-down + 5 changing stations = 59 minutes. Lower-volume option uses two sets throughout: 32 strength minutes, 50 total. Cardio days = 5 warm-up + 15/25/45 moderate walking + 3 cool-down + 2 setup = 25/35/55 minutes. Sunday has no required workout. Three five-minute moderate blocks on gym days plus the three cardio days give 60/90/150 moderate minutes per week.

Minute allocations are estimates. Queues, unfamiliar equipment, longer rests and video study can extend a session. Shorten the final cardio block to respect the hour; if more time is needed, omit the final assistance exercise rather than rushing technique or removing needed rest. No recommendation to fill recovery days with an hour of activity. No exercise calorie burn is inferred or added to the food budget.

## Body-composition goal and nutrition

The structured `goals.trainingPlan` records the explicit fat-loss/muscle objective and reported experience. A provisional 0.4–0.7 kg/week range is a conservative planning choice within/near [CDC gradual-loss guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html), not a personalized prediction. Monitor consistent weigh-ins over 2–4 weeks, optional waist measurements, and actual weights/reps. Do not invent body-fat percentage, waist, working loads or muscle gains.

[Energy-deficit meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.14075) found lean-mass gains were reduced during energy deficiency; its group-level findings do not establish an exact personal threshold. Preserve the agreed 1710-kcal target and 140-g protein target while flagging calorie needs for review under regular lifting. The existing 79-kg goal and December 10 deadline stay unchanged, with an explicit ambitious-pace note; no promise that the more gradual muscle-focused pace meets that date. The activity estimate previously excluded deliberate exercise, so do not automatically add workout burn or overwrite intake targets.
